What is this?

Got a PlayStation controller and a PC game that only wants an Xbox pad? This little program sits in between: it reads your DualShock 4 or DualSense and shows up as an Xbox 360 controller. That's it. No compiling, no fuss.

The short version

  • Works over USB and Bluetooth, reconnects by itself
  • Remap any button, save setups as profiles
  • Gyro aiming on DualSense, with auto-calibration so the camera doesn't drift
  • Rumble, light bar and adaptive triggers work back to the pad
  • Built-in test page so you can try it with just your keyboard
dualshock2xbox; a hobby project, use at your own risk.
